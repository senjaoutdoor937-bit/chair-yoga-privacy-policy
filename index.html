Privacy Policy for Chair Yoga for Seniors

Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Senja Apps handles information when you use Chair Yoga for Seniors.

Practice history and preferences

The app stores your completed routines, session dates and durations, and preferences such as text size, voice guidance settings, and weekly practice goal on your device. The app uses these records to show your session history, streak, weekly progress, and monthly recap. You can use the app without creating an account. Senja Apps does not receive your practice history or preferences from the app.

Advertising and third-party services

This version of the app contains no advertising SDK or third-party analytics. Your practice history and preferences are not shared with advertisers. The app store and your device provider may handle downloads, updates, and device backups according to their own policies and settings.

Retention and deletion

Practice history and preferences remain in the app’s local storage until you clear the app’s stored data or uninstall it. To erase the copy on your device, open your device settings, select Chair Yoga for Seniors, and clear its stored data. Device or store backup settings may govern any separate backup copies.

Contact

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond and keep a record as needed to resolve your request. For privacy questions or requests about information you shared with us, contact Senja Apps at senjaoutdoor937@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
